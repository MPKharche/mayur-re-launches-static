Overview

Festive September is live in Pune West. The launches that matter for a Baner / Balewadi 2 BHK in the ₹1.2–1.4 Cr all-in band are Bella Casa Royale Tower O (Baner–Sus) and Meridian Riverside (Balewadi, fest through 30 Sep). Hinjewadi’s real Sep launch is Ceratec Westwinds Tower C — useful as a value / IT comparator, not a Baner lifestyle substitute.

Pune East has bookable scale in Kharadi (Venkatesh New Beginnings) and Hadapsar Annexe (SP Everra). Andheri is active (Purva Estrella + Spenta Anthea) but far above the Pune 2 BHK budget. Mulund has no genuine new residential launch this fortnight. Powai’s clean RERA card is Hiranandani Rockside (ultra-luxury 4 BHK).
